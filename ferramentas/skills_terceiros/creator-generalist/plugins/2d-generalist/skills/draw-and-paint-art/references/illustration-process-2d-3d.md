# Illustration process: 3D base, paint over

How a finished illustration is built: sketch, Blender sculpt and lit clay render, then a paint-over in Clip Studio Paint
(paid; Krita is the free route, see `krita-and-tools.md`). Read from silent process clips (frames only), so each step is `seen`.
Readings of intent are marked guessed. The Blender build steps for backgrounds are in `paint-stylized-backgrounds`.

## Six stages in order

1. Idea sketch: loose line drawing with the whole composition and props as simple outlines.
2. Character sculpt in Blender (sphere, head, body).
3. Posing the finished sculpt.
4. Modeling: white blockout of props and environment.
5. Texture and lighting: a coloured render with a saturated blue-violet fill, a pink key and hard cast shadows.
6. Painting: the render is opened in Clip Studio Paint (canvas 4000 x 5382 px, 72 dpi in the title bar) and painted over with a
   pen tablet, face and eyes in view, then skin, clothes and props. The final adds film grain and sparkles.

3D carries pose, perspective and light; paint carries the face, skin and surface detail. Source:

## Paint props, not only the face

A single prop (a carton) is isolated on a blue to violet gradient and painted on its own (pink wash over the lit side, label
redrawn) before the face is painted. Check: seen.

## Line art first variant

Line art over the flower layout, then a grey clay sculpt of the girl with flower geometry in a black void, then flat colour with
dark outlines, then zoomed face paint (eye whites, lashes, blush, lips). The last pass is a colour grade: a Hue/Saturation
node mixed with Soft Light (visible in the shader editor), shown as a warm pink glow and toggled back. Source:

## Finished-piece reveals and palette pairs (second pass)

- Reveal framing: line sketch full screen, push in until blurred, land on the finished piece, pull back to the whole canvas; canvas sizes seen 4000 x 5383, 3300 x 3300 and 3000 x 2500 px at 72 dpi.
- Palette: one dominant warm hue against its opposite cool hue, such as orange silk on blue-violet sky or red dress on green forest.
- Posterised flat shading with orange window light and blue-violet fill.
