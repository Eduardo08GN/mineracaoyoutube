# CapCut

Steps for CapCut desktop and mobile, the fastest route to a captioned vertical Short. Written from CapCut's help
centre (capcut.com/help). CapCut moves features between free and Pro often and renames panels, so check each step in
the installed version. Read CapCut's current terms before commercial use of its templates, music and effects.

Reading map
- Project setup
- Cutting
- Captions and text
- Keyframes, speed and effects
- Audio
- Export

## Project setup

**Start a vertical project**
1. Import the media, drag the main clip to the timeline.
2. Set the ratio to 9:16 in the player's Ratio menu (desktop) or the Format or Aspect button (mobile).
3. Background: Canvas > Blur fills the bars behind landscape footage.
- Use when / pitfalls: set the ratio before placing text, or the text moves when the frame changes.

## Cutting

**Split, trim and delete**
1. Move the playhead, press Ctrl+B (desktop) or tap Split (mobile).
2. Select the unwanted part and delete. Ripple trimming closes the gap on the main track.
3. Auto cut of silences: look for a "Remove pauses" or similar option on the audio or under Smart tools.
- Use when / pitfalls: the main track ripples, overlay tracks do not. Lock overlays to the right clip.

## Captions and text

**Auto captions**
1. Text > Auto captions (desktop) or Captions > Auto captions (mobile). Pick the spoken language.
2. Read every line and fix names, numbers and slang by hand.
3. Apply one text style and a caption template, then set the position inside the vertical safe zone.
- Use when / pitfalls: auto captions may be a Pro feature in some versions and regions. Export the captions as SRT
  when you need them in another editor.

**Text and stickers**
1. Text > Add text, set font, stroke and shadow.
2. Animation tab: In, Out and Loop animations with a duration slider.

## Keyframes, speed and effects

**Keyframe a zoom or position**
1. Select the clip, move the playhead, click the diamond keyframe button next to Scale or Position.
2. Move the playhead, change the value. CapCut adds the next keyframe.
3. Set easing on the keyframe (the curve option) where the version offers it.

**Speed ramp**
1. Select the clip > Speed > Curve. Pick a preset (Montage, Hero, Bullet) or Custom.
2. Drag points up for fast, down for slow. Turn on smooth slow motion (frame interpolation) for slow parts.
- Use when / pitfalls: place the slowest point on the beat. Smooth slow motion needs clean footage without heavy blur.

**Effects and transitions**
1. Effects > Video effects for shakes, glitches and flashes. Drag onto the clip or an effect track.
2. Transitions sit between two clips. Keep them short (0.2 to 0.5 s) and in one family.

## Audio

**Music, beats and levels**
1. Audio > Music or import your own track. Prefer music licensed for the platform and use; for any other track, name the risk once and ask the user.
2. Beat markers: select the music > Beats or Match cut, then snap cuts to the markers.
3. Lower music under voice with the clip volume, or keyframe the volume.

## Export

**Export settings**
1. Export > Resolution 1080p, frame rate matched to the footage, format MP4, codec H.264.
2. Export captions as SRT if another tool needs them.
- Use when / pitfalls: turn off the end card or watermark if your plan shows one. Check the export with `ffprobe`.
